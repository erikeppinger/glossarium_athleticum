# Trainer guide

How to program for clients with Glossarium Athleticum: build a plan, hand it off, send updates and read your client's report.

There are no accounts and no server. You build the plan on your own device, send it as a file, and your client trains on theirs. Your client's training data never reaches you unless they choose to send it.

The [Trainee guide](trainee-guide.md) describes what your client sees.

## Build a plan

You have three ways to build a plan, and you can mix them:

- **In the app:** Plans → **New plan → Build a new plan**. This works best on Windows or Linux, with a keyboard.
- **From a spreadsheet:** fill in the plan template in Excel or Google Sheets, then use Plans → **New plan → From spreadsheet**. The template is in Settings → **Plan / library templates**. It has one row per exercise: week, day, session, order, sets, reps, weight/intensity, rest, notes and video link. Exercise names are matched against the library; anything unknown is shown to you to pick or create.
- **From a spreadsheet with pictures:** the same, zipped together with the pictures it names (**From spreadsheet + pictures (.zip)**).

Set your name under the plan's menu → **Created by**. It travels with the plan, shows on your client's device, and heads their report.

## Prescription tools

- **Percentages:** write `70%` as the weight. It resolves against each client's own estimated one-rep max, which updates as they get stronger.
- **Time and distance:** write `45 sec`, `1:30` or `250m` as reps, and the client gets a timer or a distance field.
- **Supersets and circuits:** in a session, select exercises → **Group into superset**. Uneven set counts work. Rest comes after each round.
- **Blocks:** save a warm-up, mobility circuit or finisher once (select exercises → **Save as block**) and insert it into any session. A block is copied in, not linked, so editing it later doesn't change plans you've already sent.
- **Duplicate to other weeks:** copy a session across the weeks of a block.
- **Notes and videos:** each exercise can carry your note and a video link.

Your own exercises (with cues, safety notes, photos and a video) can go into the library via the **+** on the Exercises tab. Export them for a client from the library's export menu.

## Hand off the plan

Open the plan → share icon:

- **Share as plan file (.json)**: the normal way. Send it any way you like: WhatsApp, email, Bluetooth, a USB stick.
- **Share as encrypted plan file (.tplan)**: the same plan, protected by a passphrase. Tell your client the passphrase another way, not in the same message.
- **Share as spreadsheet (.csv)**: if your client would rather read it in a spreadsheet.

Your client opens the file and the plan is theirs: they put it on their calendar and train.

## Send an update

Edit the same plan on your device and send it again, exactly like the first time.

- **If your client hasn't changed the plan,** they confirm once and their copy is replaced with your new version.
- **If they have edited it,** they see both versions and choose: keep theirs, take yours, or keep both.
- **Either way, their calendar dates, logged workouts and settings stay.** You own the contents of the plan; your client owns their dates and their training history.
- **If your new version removes a session they've already trained,** they're warned before anything is deleted.

Keep editing the plan you originally sent. A duplicate, or a plan rebuilt from scratch, counts as a different plan, and your client would get it as a second plan instead of an update.

## Read your client's report

Ask your client for **Progress → share → Share full log (PDF)**. It shows:

- prescribed vs. actual for every exercise and set;
- skipped exercises with the reason (pain or injury is shown in red) and what replaced them;
- missed sets, reps under target and near-failure RPE;
- your client's own notes per exercise;
- session adherence, volume and personal records.

## Progression suggestions

During a workout the app suggests the next weight from your client's history. The suggestions follow published resistance-training guidance: ACSM's two-for-two rule, RPE autoregulation, and deload and detraining thresholds. The sources are shown in the app. Clients can turn suggestions off in Settings.

## Platforms

You can build on Windows or Linux and your clients train on Android, using the same plan files. An iPhone version is in the works.
