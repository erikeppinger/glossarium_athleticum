# Trainee guide

How to train with Glossarium Athleticum: get a plan onto your phone, put it on your calendar, log your workouts and see your progress.

Everything stays on your device. There is no account and no sign-up.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your next scheduled session, missed exercises to make up, quick actions (start a session, log an activity, random workout) |
| **Exercises** | The exercise library: descriptions, safety notes, videos, your history per exercise |
| **Plans** | Your training plans: import, build, edit, archive |
| **Calendar** | When each session happens. Move, add or remove dates |
| **Progress** | Summaries, personal records, volume per muscle group, reports to share |

Settings is the gear icon in the top-right corner of every tab.

## Get a plan

**From your trainer.** Your trainer sends you a plan file (`.json`, or `.tplan` if it's encrypted). Open it with the app, or share it to the app from WhatsApp, email or Bluetooth. You can also go to **Plans → New plan → From plan file**. For a `.tplan` file, your trainer gives you the passphrase separately.

**Build your own.** Go to **Plans → New plan → Build a new plan**, or tap **Add to plan** on any exercise in the library.

**From a spreadsheet.** Fill in the plan template (Settings → Plan / library templates) in Excel or Google Sheets, then use **Plans → New plan → From spreadsheet**. The same menu offers the blank template (**Need a blank template first?**).

## Updates from your trainer

When your trainer changes your plan, they send you the file again. Open it like the first time.

- **You haven't changed the plan yourself:** the app asks once: *Update "Plan name"?* Tap **Update**. Your calendar dates and logged workouts stay as they are.
- **You changed the plan on your phone:** the app shows both versions side by side. Choose one:
  - **Keep this device's version**: ignore this update. The next update will ask again.
  - **Use updated version**: take your trainer's version. Your dates and workouts stay.
  - **Keep both as separate plans**: keep your edited copy and add the new one. Future updates go to the new one.
- If the new version drops a session you already trained, the app warns you in red first, because that workout would be deleted.
- If you open a file you already have, the app says *Already up to date*.

## Schedule your sessions

Open a plan and tap **Add date** under a session, or use the **Calendar** tab. A session can have several dates. If you start a session on a different day than planned, the app offers to move it to today.

Workout reminders can be turned on in Settings → **Workout reminders**.

## Log a workout

Start a session from **Today**, from the plan, or from the calendar.

- Each set is pre-filled with the plan's numbers. Change what was different, then tick the set off (✓). The rest timer starts automatically.
- Ticked a set by mistake? Tap it again to edit.
- Timed exercises (planks, holds) and distances (carries, runs) show a timer or a distance field instead of reps.
- **Supersets** run round by round; the rest comes after each round.
- **Plate calculator**: the icon in the top bar, without leaving the workout.
- **Skip an exercise**: pick a reason (optional), or **Skip & replace** it with another exercise. Skipped exercises appear on the Today tab so you can make them up later (**Do now**, **Add to next session**, or dismiss).
- **A note for your trainer**: the note icon on an exercise ("left shoulder felt off"). It appears in the report.

When you're done, tap **Finish session**.

- If some sets aren't ticked off, the app tells you which ones have numbers you entered and which still show the plan's numbers. Choose **Log as done**, **Finish without them**, or **Review**.
- You then see a summary of the workout. Correct anything, then **Confirm & finish**.

Leaving a workout without finishing asks first if you've logged or typed anything. A workout in progress also survives the app closing.

Finished workouts can still be corrected: Progress → Workout history → tap a workout → tap a set.

## See your progress

The **Progress** tab shows a week, a month or a custom range:

- sessions, sets and volume, compared with the previous period;
- personal records and estimated one-rep max;
- sets per muscle group this week, with recommended ranges;
- history per exercise (tap an exercise in the library → View history).

Progress charts, streaks and PRs can be hidden in Settings (**Focus mode**) if you'd rather not see them.

## Send a report to your trainer

Progress → share icon:

- **Share as PDF**: the summary.
- **Share full log (PDF)**: every set, prescribed vs. actual, skipped exercises with the reason, and your notes. This is the one trainers want.

## Back up your data

Your data lives only on this device. To move to a new phone, or just to be safe:

- **Settings → Export full backup**: one encrypted file with everything. Choose a passphrase you will remember. It can't be recovered.
- **Settings → Restore from backup**: replaces everything on this device with the backup. If the file is damaged, nothing is changed.

## Privacy

No account, no ads, no tracking. The app only goes online if you turn on embedded YouTube videos for an exercise. See the [Privacy Policy](../PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
