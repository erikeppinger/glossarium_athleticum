Training App

Plan it, train it, see it work — without handing your data to anyone.

A local-first strength & conditioning planner for Android. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

The idea

Most training apps make you choose between a real programme and a usable phone experience: either you keep a spreadsheet that no gym floor is comfortable with, or you use an app that wants a subscription, an account, and your workout history on someone else’s server.

This app takes the spreadsheet you already have, turns it into something you can actually train from, and keeps every byte on your own device.

What it does

Bring your own programme

Import a plan from a spreadsheet or plan file. Unrecognised exercise names are never silently guessed — you resolve each one, or add it as a custom exercise.

An exercise library that explains itself

Descriptions, cues, safety notes and reference videos. Search, filter by category or muscle area, or by who created an exercise — you, or a plan you imported.

Built for the gym floor

Execution mode logs every set inline with big touch targets. A rest timer runs between sets and follows you across the app, so checking something mid-rest doesn’t lose it.

Schedule how you like

Drop sessions onto a calendar in any order. Completed days are marked. Optional reminders on training days, plus a weekly overview of what’s planned.

Progress you can read at a glance

Week or month summaries — sessions completed, volume, personal records — with swipeable trend charts and a per-exercise drill-down into weight over time.

Track the body, if you want to

Optional bodyweight and measurement tracking over time. Off unless you ask for it — plenty of people train without it, and the app works the same either way.

Your data stays yours. Everything lives in a database on your phone. There is no account and no server — the release build does not request internet permission at all. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose (Argon2id + AES-256-GCM) and go wherever you put them. Sharing a plan with a training partner uses the normal Android share sheet — it happens because you tapped share, never in the background. The only outbound links are the reference-video URLs you tap yourself, which open in your browser.

Details worth noticing

DetailWhy it’s there
Rest timer survives leaving the screen Stored as a fixed end time, not a ticking counter — it can’t drift or reset when the app is backgrounded, and it vibrates when rest is up.
Personal records detected automatically Compared against your full history plus earlier sets in the same session, so a PR is flagged the moment you hit it.
Progression suggestions Reads your recent sessions and suggests a next load — including recognising a plateau or a missed target rather than pushing weight blindly.
Colour-blind-safe palettes Muscle areas are colour-coded across the app, with Okabe–Ito and Paul Tol schemes and a hue-free option. Colour is always paired with a text label, never the only signal.
kg or lbs, Monday or Sunday Weight is stored in kilograms and converted only for display, so switching units never rewrites or corrupts your history.
Plans stay editable Change sets, reps, rest or notes on an existing session; reorder or remove exercises. A programme you follow for months is one you’ll want to adjust.
Deleting never costs you history by accident Archive a plan to hide it losslessly, or delete it — in which case the app tells you exactly what would go (“12 logged workouts, 148 sets, March to August”) and offers to keep the training history regardless.

Where it stands

The app is feature-complete for personal use and has been exercised end-to-end with a real nine-block strength programme — imported from a spreadsheet, cross-referenced against a second source for exercise descriptions and video links, scheduled, trained, and reviewed.
