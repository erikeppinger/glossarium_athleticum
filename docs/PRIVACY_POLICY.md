# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-10-15**

This app is local-first: it works entirely on your device, does not require an account, and does not send your data anywhere - with one narrow, opt-in exception (embedded video, see below) that doesn't involve the developer either. This policy explains exactly what that means.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK, and no server of any kind. The developer never receives any information about you or your use of the app.

## Embedded video (opt-in, Android only)

If you turn on embedded playback for a specific exercise's video (see the Disclaimer's "Embedded video" section for what that means and who's responsible for what), your device loads that video directly from YouTube when you view it - the same as opening the video link in your browser would, just without leaving the app. This is the one feature in the app that talks to a network at all, and it never routes through any server operated by this app; YouTube/Google receives the request the same way it would from any browser, subject to their own privacy policy, not this one. It's off by default for every video, on every exercise, until someone explicitly turns it on.

## What's stored, and where

Everything you enter - training plans, the exercise library (including any exercise photos you attach), logged workouts, body measurements, and settings - is stored only in this app's private storage area on your device. None of it leaves your device unless you deliberately export or share it yourself.

## Backups and exports

You can export a full encrypted backup, or a single plan, to a file. These files are protected by a passphrase you choose; only you hold that passphrase, and the developer has no way to read a backup file even if they had a copy of it. Where you save or send that file afterwards (device storage, a cloud drive, a message to someone) is entirely up to you and is covered by that destination's own privacy policy, not this one.

## Sharing

When you use the app's "Share" action (for a plan, a backup file, or a progress summary), your device's own share sheet opens and hands the file or text directly to whichever app or contact you choose. That exchange happens between your device and the destination you pick - it does not pass through any server operated by this app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** - used only if you turn on workout reminders or the weekly overview in Settings. You can disable this at any time.
- **Photos / files** - used only when you choose to attach a photo to a custom exercise, or pick a file to import/restore. The app only accesses the specific file you select via the system picker.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, so there is nothing to collect from children specifically. It is not directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and the new version will ship with the app and be posted at the same web address.
